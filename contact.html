✳ A few quick answers

Wondering about something?

Is Luma a course platform?

This version is a lightweight, static creative-learning website: a place to explore ideas and inspiration. It does not yet manage lessons, accounts, or course progress.

Can I suggest a gallery item?

Absolutely. Use the form above to share the idea. The form still needs to be connected to a delivery service before it can receive messages.

Do I need experience to start?

No. The whole point is that curiosity is enough. Start with an image or topic that catches your attention.

Can I contribute to the website?

You can extend the plain HTML and CSS pages, swap in your own licensed media, and connect a form or account backend when you are ready.
